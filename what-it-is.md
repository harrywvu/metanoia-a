# VIBE Video Processor

## What It Does

Upload a short video of a person moving. Get back a 3D animation of that movement.

## Why It's Useful

No expensive motion capture suits. No special cameras. Just a regular video.

## How It Works

1. You upload a video (5-10 seconds)
2. The system analyzes each frame
3. You get a 3D model that you can view from any angle

## What You Can Do With It

- Watch the movement from different camera angles
- Download the 3D animation
- Get a notification on Discord when processing is done

## Real Uses

- Game developers creating character animations
- Students learning about 3D motion capture
- Anyone curious about turning video into 3D movement
